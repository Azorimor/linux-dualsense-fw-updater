Supported Hardware & Board Revisions

DualSense and DualSense Edge USB IDs, BDM board revision decoding, Sony firmware targets, and colorway detection.

The updater inspects Feature Report 0x20 (hw_info and fw_series) along with the USB Product ID to determine your controller's mainboard revision and matching Sony firmware target.

BDM-010 Launch Controllers

Launch-era BDM-010 DualSense controllers are not supported by Sony's official PC firmware catalog (info.json). The application detects BDM-010 and disables official catalog flashing for safety.

Serial Number & Colorway Detection

When the controller responds to Feature Report 0x80 / 0x81, the updater reads the controller serial number (when permissions allow) and performs best-effort colorway identification from serial characters [4..6] (covering standard finishes such as White, Midnight Black, Cosmic Red, Nova Pink, Galactic Purple, Starlight Blue, Chroma variants, and limited editions).
