Unofficial Linux DualSense Firmware Updater

An unofficial, community-maintained Linux app for checking and updating PlayStation DualSense and DualSense Edge controller firmware over a wired USB cable. This project has no affiliation, endorsement, or relation to Sony Interactive Entertainment or PlayStation.

DownloadRead Documentation

Wired USB Controller Check

Plug in your controller with a USB cable to view its current firmware version, model, shell color, and battery status right away.

Automatic Hardware Detection

Automatically detects your controller's hardware, picks the matching official firmware update, and blocks unsupported early launch models.

Guided Safety & Recovery

Watches for accidental cable unplugs before updating starts, confirms you are ready before making changes, and explains any errors in plain English.

Simple 4-Step Update Workflow

Step-by-step screenshots of the Unofficial Linux DualSense Firmware Updater from connecting over USB through choosing an update and confirming before changes begin.

Unofficial Community Project with No Sony Affiliation

This app is an unofficial, community-maintained tool with no affiliation, endorsement, or relation to Sony Interactive Entertainment or PlayStation. Updating firmware can permanently damage your controller, and once an update starts it cannot be undone. Only use this tool if you have no access to Sony's official Windows updater or a PlayStation 5 console. Standard DualSense controllers are tested; DualSense Edge remains untested and is clearly flagged before updating.
