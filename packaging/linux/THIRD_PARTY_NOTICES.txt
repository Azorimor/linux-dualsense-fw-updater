3. AppImage Dynamic Linking & User Relinking Rights (LGPL Compliance)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Qt 6, KDE Kirigami, `hidapi`, and bundled system libraries are dynamically
linked or dynamically loaded as shared objects (`.so`) inside the AppImage.

In accordance with LGPL-3.0 Section 4 and LGPL-2.1 Section 6, you may inspect,
modify, replace, or relink any bundled shared library or QML module inside the
AppImage:

1. Extract the AppImage contents:
     ./linux-dualsense-fw-updater-<tag>-x86_64.AppImage --appimage-extract
2. Replace or update shared libraries / plugins / QML modules inside:
     squashfs-root/usr/lib/
     squashfs-root/usr/plugins/
     squashfs-root/usr/qml/
3. Run the extracted application directly via:
     ./squashfs-root/AppRun
   or repackage with `appimagetool`.
